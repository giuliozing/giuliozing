### About

I am an MSc student in Computer Science at ETH Zürich (Major in Machine Intelligence), working on the
**security and reliability of AI agents**. I am currently a Visiting Student Researcher at **MIT**,
and was previously at the **University of Cambridge**, where I worked on securing Computer-Use Agents.
