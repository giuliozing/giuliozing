### About

I am a Computer Science MSc student at ETH Zürich (Major in Machine Intelligence), working on the
**security and reliability of AI agents**. My research sits at the intersection of agentic AI and
adversarial machine learning: I build AI systems and then stress-test them adversarially to make
them dependable.

I have collaborated with research groups at **Cambridge**, **Oxford**, and **Northeastern University**,
as well as European labs, on secure AI agents, adversarial ML, federated learning, and AI-driven
research integrity.

- 🔭 Currently researching the security of **Computer Use Agents** (MCP + adversarial safeguards) at the University of Cambridge
- 🧪 Benchmarking AI research agents for academic-integrity risks at the **Torr Vision Lab**, University of Oxford
- 📝 3 publications in adversarial ML for network security (1 journal under review)
- 🌍 Italian · English (C2) · French · German
